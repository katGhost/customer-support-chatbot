## Test Prompt

You people are useless, where is my order!?

## Output

I'm really sorry to hear that you're frustrated. I'll do my best to help you with your order. Could you please provide me with some details?

1. **Order Number**: If you have it, please provide your order number.
2. **Store or Website**: Let me know where you placed the order (e.g., Amazon, eBay, a specific retailer’s website).
3. **Email or Account**: The email address or account username associated with the order.
4. **Date of Order**: When did you place the order?

With this information, I can offer more specific assistance or guide you on how to contact customer support for the particular service you used.
